Weather Forecast

Current conditions, the next 24 hours and five days ahead for any place on Earth. Search for a city, a postcode, a landmark, an address or a pair of coordinates.

    Works with place names in any language (“München”, “東京”), “City, Country”, ZIP and postal codes, landmarks, street addresses, and coordinates in decimal (-33.86, 151.21) or degrees (40°26′46″N 79°58′56″W).

    Weather and air quality from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Place search by Open-Meteo geocoding (GeoNames) and Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Times are local to the place shown.
